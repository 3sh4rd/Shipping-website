Last updated: 26 July 2026

Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly — such as your name, email, phone number, company and shipment details — when you request a quote, contact us or track a shipment. We also collect standard technical data (IP address, browser type, pages visited) to operate and improve the site.

How we use your information

  • To respond to quote requests and enquiries
  • To provide and manage shipping services
  • To improve our website and services
  • To send service updates and, with consent, marketing

Sharing

We share information only with carriers, customs authorities and service providers as needed to deliver your shipment, or where required by law. We do not sell your personal data.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. Contact hello@meridianimports.com.

Cookies

We use essential cookies to run the site and optional analytics cookies to understand usage. You can control cookies through your browser settings.
