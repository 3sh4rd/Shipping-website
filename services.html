Services

End-to-end import & shipping services

Everything you need to move goods across borders under one roof — freight, customs, storage and delivery.

⛵

Ocean freight

Full-container-load (FCL) and less-than-container-load (LCL) shipping from every major port. Competitive rates, reliable schedules and consolidation options that lower your per-unit cost.

  • FCL & LCL container shipping
  • Port-to-port and door-to-door
  • Reefer and out-of-gauge cargo
✈

Air freight

When speed matters, our air cargo network delivers. Choose expedited, standard or consolidated air freight with full tracking and priority handling.

  • Express & economy air options
  • Perishable & high-value cargo
  • Airport-to-door delivery
📋

Customs clearance

Our licensed customs brokers handle classification, duties, tariffs and documentation so your goods clear quickly and compliantly — no costly hold-ups.

  • Licensed customs brokerage
  • Duty & tariff calculation
  • Bonded entry & compliance
🏠

Warehousing & fulfilment

Bonded and general-order storage near key ports, with pick-and-pack, inventory management and order fulfilment to keep your supply chain moving.

  • Bonded & general storage
  • Pick, pack & fulfilment
  • Real-time inventory
🚛

Door-to-door delivery

Coordinated first- and last-mile trucking means one partner, one invoice and one point of contact for the entire import journey.

  • First- & last-mile trucking
  • Scheduled & express delivery
  • Single point of contact
💰

Cargo insurance

Flexible all-risk coverage protects your shipments against loss or damage in transit, with fast, fair claims handling.

  • All-risk cargo cover
  • Per-shipment or annual
  • Fast claims support
