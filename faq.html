FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about importing and shipping with Meridian.

How do I get an import shipping quote?

Fill out our quote form with your origin, destination, freight mode and cargo details. We'll email an all-in rate — covering freight, customs and delivery — within one business day.

\n
What's the difference between FCL and LCL?

FCL (full container load) means your goods fill an entire container, ideal for larger volumes. LCL (less than container load) shares container space with other shippers, so you only pay for the space you use — perfect for smaller shipments.

\n
How long does ocean freight take?

Transit time depends on the route. As a guide, Asia to the US West Coast is roughly 15–25 days port to port, plus customs clearance and inland delivery. We give you an estimated timeline with every quote.

\n
Who handles customs clearance?

Our licensed customs brokers handle classification, duties, tariffs and all documentation on your behalf, so your goods clear quickly and compliantly.

\n
Can I track my shipment?

Yes. Every booking comes with a tracking number you can enter on our tracking page to see live milestones from collection to delivery.

\n
What does a quote include?

Our quotes are all-in: freight charges, customs brokerage, applicable duties and taxes where known, and last-mile delivery. We flag anything variable up front so there are no surprises.

\n
Do you offer cargo insurance?

Yes. We offer flexible all-risk cargo insurance on a per-shipment or annual basis, with fast claims support if anything goes wrong in transit.

\n
What items can't you ship?

We can't handle prohibited or hazardous goods that violate international regulations or destination-country import rules. If you're unsure about a commodity, ask us before booking.
