About us

Import specialists you can rely on

Meridian Imports was founded to make global importing simple, transparent and stress-free for growing businesses.

Since 2011 we've helped over 900 companies bring their products to market — from first-time importers shipping a single pallet to established brands moving thousands of containers a year. We combine a global carrier network with licensed customs expertise and modern tracking technology, so you always know where your cargo is and what it will cost.

What we stand for

  • Transparency. All-in quotes and no surprise fees, ever.
  • Reliability. A 98.6% on-time delivery record across 120+ countries.
  • Partnership. A named account manager who knows your business.
